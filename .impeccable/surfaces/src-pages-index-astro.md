Scope: whole site (home, portfolio, about, contact, blog). Visitor mode: Experience (home, portfolio), Persuade (about, contact).
Audience: photo editors, federations, event organizers deciding whether to hire. Job: see real work fast, start a WhatsApp conversation.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is Inaldo's pitchside accreditation: the photographer's bib and press credential, not a gallery template. Refuses the dark full-bleed hero + 3-column grid default.

OWN-WORLD: Canon black (#0b0b0c ground, #141416 panels) and Canon red (#cc0000) used as solid bib fields carrying white condensed type, never as thin accents. All-sharp corners. Archivo variable (width axis 62-125, weight 100-900) for bib lettering and body; Martian Mono only for real data (counts, frame numbers, zones). Credential grammar: zone codes per discipline, access rows, issued-card layouts.

STORY: Visitor sees every photo at once, understands breadth across 17 disciplines and the press names, taps one discipline zone to inspect depth, then taps "Hire Inaldo" (WhatsApp).

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full 100dvh draggable wall of all ~580 thumbnails filling in a burst sequence from the center outward. Bottom-left: red bib block with PHOTO, INALDO PÉREZ in wide-black Archivo, one-line credit, primary "Hire Inaldo" + secondary "Portfolio". Bottom: one unbroken zone row of disciplines with counts; tapping pans the wall to that zone and dims the rest.

FORM: Press credential / pitchside bib, position 5 of 7 on grounded list, seed key 147caed9. Raises: single unbroken zone row that scales and never wraps (from drum-machine step row); type width/weight mapped to moment intensity (from telop field).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
